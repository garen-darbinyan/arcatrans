FAQ

Questions shippers ask us

How fast will I get a quote?

Most quote requests receive a firm rate within 15 minutes during business hours. Urgent? Call dispatch 24/7.

Is my cargo insured?

Yes. Every load moves under our cargo and liability insurance. Certificates of insurance are available on request.

Do you handle cross-border shipments?

Yes. We move freight between the US, Canada and Mexico and coordinate with your customs broker on ACE/ACI e-manifests.

How do I track my shipment?

Use the tracking form above, or contact your dedicated rep. We also send proactive updates at pickup, in transit and delivery.

What payment terms do you offer?

Established shippers can qualify for net terms after a quick credit review. Contact us to set up an account.

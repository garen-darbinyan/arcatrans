Privacy policy

How we handle information you share through arcatrans.com.

Last updated: October 2026

ARPI Transport Inc. (dba ARCA) and Arcatrans Inc. (“ARCA”, “we”) respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

When you submit a form or email us, we receive the details you provide — such as name, company, email, phone number, shipment information, or for job applicants, work history. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

To respond to your request, prepare quotes, arrange and track shipments, evaluate carrier partners and job applications, invoice for services, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

Website forms are delivered to our inboxes by a third-party form-processing service, and shipment information may be shared with drivers, partner carriers, customs brokers and border authorities as needed to move your freight.

Retention & security

We keep information only as long as needed for these purposes or as required by law, and protect it with reasonable safeguards.

Your rights

You may request access to or correction of your personal information, or withdraw consent, by emailing info@arcatrans.com.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Arcatrans ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through arcatrans.com and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a quote, shipment status or driver application form, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, company, phone number, email address and shipment information. Our web server also records standard technical data such as your IP address.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request, provide transportation services, evaluate driver applications and keep records required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate our business (for example, email hosting) and when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing dispatch@arcatrans.com.
